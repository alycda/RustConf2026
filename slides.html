01 — Why is this hard?

An FFI signature is a treaty between two runtimes that disagree about memory, types, errors, and encoding — and neither side can enforce it.

01 — Why is this hard?

Four disagreements

03

01 — Why is this hard?

“It's just a string”

  • rustUTF-8; CString refuses interior NUL — validation at the boundary
  • cbytes + NUL terminator, no promises whatsoever
  • swiftUTF-8 inside (since Swift 5); the NSString bridge is UTF-16 — hidden work
  • jvmmodified UTF-8 via JNI. Not quite UTF-8. Really.
  • pythonunicode object; you .encode() first — explicit, honest
  • dartUTF-16 code units; toNativeUtf8() — and you free it
